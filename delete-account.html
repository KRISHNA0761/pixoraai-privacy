PixoraAI Account Deletion

This page explains how PixoraAI users can request deletion of their PixoraAI account and associated account data.

Request Account Deletion

If you want to delete your PixoraAI account, send an account deletion request to our support email.

Please send the request from the email address associated with your PixoraAI account when possible.

Email: kv050845@gmail.com

What Will Be Deleted?

When an account deletion request is verified and processed, PixoraAI will delete the associated PixoraAI/Firebase Authentication account and applicable account data.

PixoraAI does not currently maintain a user-accessible cloud gallery for generated images.

Images that users have already saved directly to their Android device remain under the user's control and are not automatically deleted from the device.

Third-Party Services

PixoraAI uses third-party services for authentication, advertising, and AI image processing.

Some information processed by third-party service providers may be subject to their own privacy policies and retention requirements.

PixoraAI will handle deletion of account data under its control and, where applicable, request deletion from service providers according to their available processes.
